## Decision (Stefano, 2026-09-30)

Before a *different* vault replaces the session, the dirty tabs of every column are reviewed with
the quit's own machinery (ADR-0073: `QuitReview`, `QuitReviewAlert`, `saveForQuit`): «Salva /
Salva tutto», «Non salvare», «Annulla». «Annulla» (or a save that fails / a conflicted tab left
unresolved) leaves vault A open and untouched. Otherwise `columns` is reset and the new vault's
tabs are restored, so no tab of A survives the switch. Reopening the same vault changes nothing.
`restoreVersion` refuses a previous-vault tab like `saveTab` does (defence in depth).
